PORTFOLIO DASHBOARD 4.1

Privacy Policy

Effective September 7, 2026

Overview

Portfolio Dashboard 4.1 is designed to let users track portfolio positions, market values, allocations and related financial information on their Mac. The app does not require an account with the developer.

Data entered by you

Portfolio information that you enter or import into the app, such as asset names, symbols, quantities, prices, notes and portfolio values, is stored locally on your device unless you explicitly export it. The developer does not receive your portfolio data.

Market data

The app may connect to external market-data services to retrieve current or recent pricing and quote information for supported symbols. These requests may transmit technical connection information such as your IP address to the relevant data provider. The developer does not use these requests to build advertising profiles or track users across apps or websites.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

Portfolio Dashboard 4.1 does not use advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party advertising SDKs. The developer does not sell personal data.

Exports and files

If you use features such as CSV, PDF or screenshot export, the resulting files are created at your request and saved or shared using locations and services you choose. Those files remain under your control.

Retention and deletion

Local app data can be removed by deleting or resetting the relevant data in the app, or by deleting the app from your Mac. The developer does not maintain a server-side copy of your portfolio data.

Contact

For privacy questions about Portfolio Dashboard 4.1, email c.dafonte@me.com.

This policy applies to Portfolio Dashboard 4.1 for macOS.
