Support

Help and contact information for Portfolio Dashboard 4.1 for macOS.

Contact

Email c.dafonte@me.com. Please include your macOS version and a short description of the issue.

Market data

Portfolio Dashboard 4.1 retrieves market information from external data providers. Availability and update timing may depend on those services and on your internet connection.

Portfolio data

Your portfolio information is intended to remain on your Mac unless you explicitly export it. Keep regular backups of important portfolio files and exports.

CSV and PDF

If an import or export does not work as expected, include the file type and a brief description when contacting support. Do not send sensitive financial information unless necessary for troubleshooting.
