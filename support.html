SIGNAL STORM

Support

Help with Signal Storm for iPhone.

Contact

Email c.dafonte@me.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and a short description of the issue.

Data does not update

Check your internet connection and pull down on the Status screen to refresh. NOAA services can occasionally be delayed or temporarily unavailable.

Aurora Replay is loading

The replay retrieves multiple hourly images. On slower connections, allow a few seconds for the timeline to populate.

Location

Location is optional. If access is unavailable, select a city manually in the Aurora Radar. Permission can be changed under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.

Notifications

Signal Storm checks the chosen threshold after the app refreshes. It does not provide continuous server-side push monitoring while the app is closed.
